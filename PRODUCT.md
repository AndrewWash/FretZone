# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Self-taught guitarists practicing alone — laptop or desktop open near their instrument, often late at night. No teacher in the room. They're running drills, not browsing. They arrived with a specific skill to sharpen and want the tool to stay out of their way.

## Product Purpose

FretZone is a set of ear-training and fretboard practice tools for guitarists. The three core tools — Fretboard Quiz, Interval Memorization, and Tuner — build three interlocking skills: the ear-to-hand reflex of hearing a note and finding it instantly, a deeper grasp of music theory and intervals, and the confidence to play without looking at their hands. These reinforce each other; none is the single goal.

## Brand Personality

Focused and no-nonsense. Like a practice room with good acoustics and no distractions. The tool trusts the user to know what they're here for. It doesn't congratulate or cheer — it just runs clean reps.

Three-word summary: **precise, patient, minimal**.

## Anti-references

- **Duolingo and gamified learning apps** — no streaks, XP bars, mascots, reward explosions, or progress celebrations.
- **Guitar Center and music retailer sites** — no gear vibes, product grids, or promotional energy.
- **Generic SaaS dashboards** — no sidebar nav, metric cards, blue gradient hero banners, or enterprise layout conventions.
- **Neon / synthwave music apps** — no glowing edges, retro scanline grids, neon palettes, or DJ aesthetic.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice over polish.** The tool exists to build skill. Visual choices that don't serve the practice workflow are overhead.
2. **Respect the musician.** Users are capable learners, not beginners who need hand-holding. The UI can be sparse and direct without being cold.
3. **Minimal ceremony.** Get the user into a practice session as fast as possible. Configuration is a means, not an end.
4. **Feedback, not reward.** Signal whether the user was right or wrong with clarity. Don't dress it up as achievement.
5. **Consistent quiet.** The same uncluttered aesthetic everywhere. Nothing should pop, flash, or demand attention unless it's the pitch feedback itself.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Includes focus management, color contrast ratios, and ARIA attributes. The tool is used hands-on (guitar in hand) — interactions should be keyboard-navigable and not require fine mouse control during practice. No specific reduced-motion or color blindness accommodations required beyond standard AA compliance.
